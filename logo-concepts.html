Logo Concepts

Five distinct directions for the North Shore Projects brand mark. Each designed to work at every size — from a 32px favicon to a van wrap.

Concept 01

The Compass

A stylised north arrow integrated with a roofline. The upward-pointing form says "North" while the angled base reads as a house roof. Directional, purposeful, premium. The arrow motif suggests progress and precision — pointing the way forward.

The Monogram

An interlocking "N" and "S" in a bold geometric style — the strokes overlap and interweave like construction beams. Luxury brand energy meets trades confidence. The letters form a memorable shape that works as a standalone icon. Housed in the signature rounded square.

Concept 05

The Craftsman's Mark

A hexagonal badge with "NSP" at its centre — like a quality stamp or hallmark pressed into finished work. The hexagon is a universal symbol of precision (think nuts, bolts, honeycomb strength). The outer ring and inner text feel like a seal of approval. Something you'd be proud to see stamped on your project.
