Sydney's Trusted Cleaning Professionals

At North Shore Projects, we deliver exceptional cleaning services that exceed expectations. Our professional cleaning team uses industry-leading equipment and eco-friendly products to ensure your space is not just clean, but hygienically spotless.

Whether you need a thorough end-of-lease clean to get your bond back, a deep clean to refresh your home, or regular cleaning services to maintain your property, we've got the expertise and attention to detail to deliver outstanding results every time.

  • End of Lease Cleaning Comprehensive bond cleaning that meets real estate standards, with a bond-back guarantee for your peace of mind.
  • Deep Cleaning Intensive cleaning for homes that need extra attention, covering every surface, corner, and hard-to-reach area.
  • Commercial Cleaning Professional cleaning services for offices, retail spaces, and commercial properties with flexible scheduling.
  • Post-Construction Cleaning Thorough cleanup after renovations or construction work, removing dust, debris, and building residue.
  • Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning Professional steam cleaning for carpets, rugs, and upholstered furniture to remove stains and allergens.
