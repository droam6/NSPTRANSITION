What We Offer

Sydney's Trusted Cleaning Professionals

At North Shore Projects, we deliver exceptional cleaning services that exceed expectations. Our professional cleaning team uses industry-leading equipment and eco-friendly products to ensure your space is not just clean, but hygienically spotless.

Whether you need a thorough end-of-lease clean to get your bond back, a deep clean to refresh your home, or regular cleaning services to maintain your property, we've got the expertise and attention to detail to deliver outstanding results every time.

  • End of Lease Cleaning Comprehensive bond cleaning that meets real estate standards, with a bond-back guarantee for your peace of mind.
  • Deep Cleaning Intensive cleaning for homes that need extra attention, covering every surface, corner, and hard-to-reach area.
  • Commercial Cleaning Professional cleaning services for offices, retail spaces, and commercial properties with flexible scheduling.
  • Post-Construction Cleaning Thorough cleanup after renovations or construction work, removing dust, debris, and building residue.
  • Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning Professional steam cleaning for carpets, rugs, and upholstered furniture to remove stains and allergens.

Common Questions

Cleaning FAQ

Yes, our end-of-lease cleaning comes with a bond-back guarantee. If your real estate agent identifies any cleaning issues within 72 hours of our clean, we'll return and address them at no additional cost.

Our deep cleaning covers every surface in your home: kitchen appliances (inside and out), bathrooms, floors, skirting boards, window sills, light switches, door frames, and all hard-to-reach areas. We'll provide a detailed checklist specific to your property.

We use professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products that are safe for families, pets, and the environment. For specialist tasks like oven cleaning or mould removal, we use targeted products that are highly effective while remaining non-toxic.

A standard 2–3 bedroom home takes approximately 4–6 hours for a deep clean, and 6–8 hours for an end-of-lease clean. Larger properties or those requiring extra attention may take longer. We'll give you an accurate time estimate in your quote.

We recommend a deep clean every 3–6 months to maintain a healthy home environment. For busy households or those with pets, a monthly or fortnightly maintenance clean can help keep on top of things between deep cleans.
