Trusted Cleaning Services for Every Home

At North Shore Projects, we deliver professional cleaning services tailored to the unique needs of North Shore Sydney homeowners and tenants. Whether you are moving out and need your bond back, preparing a home for new occupants, or simply want a sparkling clean living environment, our experienced cleaning team has you covered. We use eco-friendly products and commercial-grade equipment to ensure a thorough, streak-free clean every single time.

Our cleaners are fully insured, police-checked and trained to the highest industry standards. We understand that your home is your sanctuary, which is why we treat every property with the utmost care and respect. From Chatswood to Mosman and everywhere in between, North Shore Projects is the name trusted by hundreds of local families for reliable, detail-oriented cleaning services.

End of Lease Cleaning

Moving out can be stressful enough without worrying about getting your bond back. Our end of lease cleaning service is designed to meet the strict requirements of real estate agents and property managers across the North Shore. We offer a comprehensive bond-back guarantee, meaning if your agent is not satisfied with the clean, we will return at no extra charge to address any concerns. Our end of lease package covers every room from top to bottom, including walls, skirting boards, light fittings, cupboard interiors, carpets and all wet areas.

Deep House Cleaning

Sometimes a regular clean is not enough. Our deep house cleaning service goes beyond surface-level tidying to tackle the dirt, grime and buildup that accumulates in hard-to-reach areas over time. We focus on sanitising bathrooms, descaling taps and shower screens, removing grease buildup from kitchen surfaces, cleaning behind and under furniture, and restoring your home to a level of freshness that makes it feel brand new.

Our Full Range of Cleaning Services

  • End of lease cleaning with a full bond-back guarantee for peace of mind when vacating your rental property
  • Deep house cleaning targeting every corner, surface and hidden area for a complete home refresh
  • Regular domestic cleaning on a weekly, fortnightly or monthly schedule to keep your home consistently spotless
  • Move-in and move-out cleaning to ensure your new home is fresh and hygienic before you settle in
  • Spring cleaning to give your home a seasonal overhaul, removing dust, allergens and stale air
  • Post-renovation cleaning to clear away construction dust, debris and residue after building works
  • Window cleaning for sparkling, streak-free glass both inside and out across all accessible windows
  • Oven and kitchen deep clean removing baked-on grease, carbon buildup and food residue from ovens, rangehoods and stovetops

No matter the size of your home or the scope of the job, our team arrives fully equipped with all the supplies and equipment needed to deliver outstanding results. We take pride in our attention to detail and consistently receive five-star feedback from clients across the North Shore. Every clean includes a final quality check and we are not finished until you are completely satisfied with the result.
