Expert Tiling Solutions for Every Space

At North Shore Projects, we specialise in delivering premium tiling services that transform your living spaces with precision craftsmanship and meticulous attention to detail. Our team of licensed and fully insured tilers brings years of experience to every project, whether it is a compact bathroom renovation or a large-scale commercial floor installation across Sydney's North Shore.

We understand that tiling is more than laying tiles on a surface. It requires careful planning, precise measurements, proper substrate preparation and expert installation techniques to ensure a long-lasting, beautiful finish. Our tilers work with all tile types including porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, marble, terrazzo, mosaic and large-format tiles, ensuring the perfect material is selected for your specific needs and budget.

Bathroom Tiling

Your bathroom is one of the most important rooms in your home, and quality tiling makes all the difference. We handle complete bathroom tiling from floor to ceiling, including feature walls, shower recesses, niches and vanity splashbacks. Our team ensures every tile is perfectly aligned with consistent grout lines, waterproof membranes are correctly applied, and all wet-area regulations are met. Whether you prefer the sleek look of large-format rectified tiles or the charm of handmade subway tiles, we deliver a finish that is both stunning and functional.

Kitchen Splashbacks

A beautifully tiled kitchen splashback can completely transform the look and feel of your kitchen. We install splashbacks in a wide variety of materials and styles, from classic subway tiles and herringbone patterns to contemporary large-format porcelain and natural stone. Our precise cutting and finishing ensure neat edges around power points, window frames and joinery, giving your kitchen a polished, professional appearance that stands the test of time.

Floor Tiling

Our floor tiling services cover all areas of your home or business, including living rooms, hallways, laundries, entryways and commercial premises. We specialise in laying large-format floor tiles with minimal lippage, ensuring a perfectly flat and level surface. We also provide underfloor heating installation beneath tiles, timber-look tile installations, and patterned floor designs that add character and style to your space.

Outdoor Tiling

Extend your living space outdoors with professionally installed outdoor tiles. We use slip-resistant, frost-proof and UV-stable tiles suitable for patios, pool surrounds, balconies, driveways and alfresco dining areas. Our outdoor tiling solutions are designed to withstand the Australian climate while maintaining an attractive appearance for years to come. We also ensure proper drainage falls and expansion joints are incorporated into every outdoor tiling project.

Tile Repairs & Restoration

Cracked, chipped or loose tiles do not always require a full re-tile. Our skilled team can replace individual damaged tiles, re-grout tired-looking grout lines, repair cracked tiles and restore your tiled surfaces to their original condition. We carefully source matching tiles and grout colours to ensure seamless repairs that blend perfectly with your existing tilework.

Waterproofing

Proper waterproofing is essential for any wet-area tiling project. Our qualified waterproofers apply Australian Standard-compliant waterproof membranes to showers, bathrooms, balconies and laundries before any tiles are laid. We issue waterproofing certificates for all wet-area work, giving you complete peace of mind and ensuring your tiling project is protected against water damage for years to come.

How Our Tiling Process Works

From your first enquiry to the final grout line, we make the process simple, transparent and stress-free.

1

Consultation
