Professional Painting Services Across North Shore Sydney

Expert Interior & Exterior Painting for Your Home

At North Shore Projects, we provide comprehensive painting services that transform homes and businesses across Sydney's North Shore. Our team of licensed and fully insured painters brings years of experience to every project, ensuring a finish that is both stunning and long-lasting. Whether you are refreshing a single room or repainting your entire property, we deliver results that exceed expectations.

Interior Painting

Our interior painting service covers every surface inside your home. We handle walls, ceilings, trim, skirting boards, doors, and window frames with precision and care. We understand that your home is your sanctuary, which is why our painters work meticulously to protect your furniture and flooring while delivering a flawless, even coat. From bedrooms and living areas to kitchens and bathrooms, we use premium low-VOC paints that are safe for your family and provide a durable, beautiful finish that withstands the demands of everyday life.

Exterior Painting

Sydney's North Shore homes face constant exposure to the elements, and a quality exterior paint job is your property's first line of defence. We specialise in painting weatherboards, brick, rendered surfaces, fences, decks, pergolas, and eaves. Our team uses high-performance exterior coatings from trusted Australian brands like Dulux and Taubmans that resist fading, peeling, and moisture damage. We pay close attention to surface preparation, ensuring that every coat adheres properly and stands up to the harsh Australian sun, coastal salt air, and seasonal rain.

Colour Consultation Service

Choosing the right colour palette can feel overwhelming. That is why every painting project with North Shore Projects includes a complimentary colour consultation. Our experienced team works with you to select colours that complement your architecture, interior design, and personal style. We consider natural lighting, room dimensions, existing furnishings, and neighbourhood character to recommend palettes that will enhance your property's appeal and value.

Surface Preparation

A lasting paint job starts with thorough preparation. Our process includes sanding rough surfaces, scraping peeling or flaking old paint, filling cracks and holes with quality filler, and applying premium primers to ensure strong adhesion. For exterior work, we pressure wash surfaces to remove dirt, mildew, and loose debris before any paint is applied. Proper preparation is the foundation of every project we undertake, and it is the reason our finishes last years longer than the competition.

Specialty Finishes & Feature Walls

Looking to make a bold design statement? We offer specialty finishes including feature walls, textured coatings, suede and metallic effects, limewash, and decorative techniques that add character and depth to any room. Whether you want a modern accent wall in your living area or an elegant textured finish in your dining room, our painters have the skills and tools to bring your vision to life.

Commercial Painting

Beyond residential work, North Shore Projects delivers professional commercial painting for offices, retail spaces, strata properties, and common areas. We work around your business hours to minimise disruption and ensure a professional finish that represents your brand. Our commercial team is experienced with large-scale projects and understands the unique requirements of body corporate and strata management.

Our Painting Process

From consultation to final inspection, we follow a proven four-step process to ensure every painting project is completed to the highest standard.

Colour Consultation

We help you choose the perfect colours for your space, taking into account lighting, architecture, and your personal preferences to create a palette you will love.

Surface Preparation

Proper prep for a flawless finish. We sand, fill, prime, and clean all surfaces to ensure the paint adheres correctly and lasts for years to come.

Professional Application

Expert painters using premium paints. We apply multiple coats with care, using brushes, rollers, and spray equipment suited to each surface for a smooth, even result.

Final Inspection

We ensure every detail is perfect. Our team conducts a thorough walkthrough with you to confirm the finished result meets your expectations before we consider the job complete.
