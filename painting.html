Sydney's Trusted Painting Professionals

At North Shore Projects, we bring colour and life to your spaces with exceptional painting services. Our skilled painters combine years of experience with premium products to deliver flawless finishes that transform homes and commercial properties alike.

We believe that great painting is about more than just coverage—it's about preparation, precision, and the right products. That's why we take time to properly prepare surfaces, use quality primers, and apply premium paints that not only look beautiful but last for years to come.

  • Interior Painting Complete interior transformations including walls, ceilings, doors, trims, and architraves with smooth, lasting finishes.
  • Exterior Painting Weather-resistant exterior painting for facades, fences, decks, and outdoor structures using durable exterior-grade paints.
  • Feature Walls & Murals Custom feature walls and accent colours to create stunning focal points in your home or business.
  • Commercial Painting Professional painting services for offices, retail spaces, and commercial properties with minimal disruption.
  • Surface Preparation Thorough surface prep including sanding, filling, priming, and repair work for perfect results.
