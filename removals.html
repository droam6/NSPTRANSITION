What We Offer

Sydney's Trusted Removalists

At North Shore Projects, we understand that moving can be one of life's most stressful experiences. That's why our dedicated removals team is committed to making your relocation as smooth and hassle-free as possible, treating your belongings with the same care we'd give our own.

Whether you're moving across the street or across Sydney, our experienced team handles every aspect of your move with professionalism and precision. From careful packing to secure transport and organised unpacking, we've got you covered every step of the way.

  • Home Relocations Complete residential moving services for apartments, houses, and units of all sizes throughout Sydney.
  • Office Relocations Efficient commercial moves with minimal business disruption, including IT equipment and office furniture.
  • Packing Services Professional packing using quality materials to ensure your items arrive safely at their destination.
  • Furniture Assembly Disassembly and reassembly of furniture items including beds, wardrobes, desks, and shelving units.
  • Heavy Item Moving Specialist equipment and expertise for pianos, safes, gym equipment, and other heavy or awkward items.

Our Process

How Your Move Works

From first call to settled in, here's what to expect.

01

Get in Touch

Tell us about your move. We'll discuss dates, locations, and any special requirements for your relocation.

02

Moving Assessment

We'll assess the scope of your move, identify any heavy or delicate items, and provide a transparent, fixed-price quote.

03

Moving Day

Our experienced team arrives on time, wraps and protects every item, and loads carefully for secure transport to your new location.

04

Unpacking & Setup

Everything placed where you want it, furniture reassembled, and boxes removed. We're not done until you're settled in.
