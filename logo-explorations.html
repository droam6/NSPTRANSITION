Two Styles, Eight Marks

Style A: bold filled arrow with roof base. Style B: thin monoline compass. Four variations of each — pick the one that feels right.

Style A — Bold Filled Arrow

Gold filled on dark. The north arrow is the roof. Solid, confident, premium.

A1

Wide Roof

The roof base is wider and more pronounced — clearly reads as a house roofline. The arrow shaft is thinner, making the roof the dominant element. The proportions say "building" first, "north" second.

Style B — Thin Monoline Compass

Minimal strokes, precise geometry. The compass needle meets the roofline. Elegant and architectural.

B1

Floor Line

The compass needle/roof mark with a horizontal line across the middle creating a "floor". Above the line: roof/attic. Below: the body of the house. Splits the shape into two zones. Still monoline, still minimal.
