Compass + House

Six ways to integrate a visible building element with the compass/north concept. All monoline, all minimal, all designed to read at 32px.

01

Compass Roof

The north arrow IS the peaked roof. A compass circle sits below as the house body. One shape, two readings — a compass pointing north and a house with a peaked roof. The arrowhead and roofline are the same stroke.

A clear house outline — peaked roof, walls, base. The chimney extends upward and terminates in a compass arrow tip, pointing north. The house is the primary read; the compass is a clever accent detail.

Compass Frame
