North Shore Projects is proud to be the local tiling specialist that Lane Cove residents trust for their bathroom and kitchen upgrades.

Bathroom Renovations for Growing Families

Lane Cove is a suburb where families settle for the long term, drawn by the excellent schools, leafy streets and strong sense of community. As families grow, bathrooms need to work harder, and outdated tiles, poor waterproofing or cramped layouts simply do not cut it. Our team specialises in complete bathroom retiling, from removing old surfaces and applying compliant waterproof membranes through to installing your chosen tiles with precision. Kitchen Splashbacks and Floor Tiling

A new kitchen splashback is one of the most cost-effective ways to refresh a tired kitchen, and Lane Cove homeowners are increasingly opting for statement designs such as herringbone patterns, textured ceramics or natural stone finishes. For floor tiling throughout living areas, hallways and laundries, we ensure a perfectly level finish using the latest levelling systems, whether you prefer the warmth of timber-look tiles or the clean lines of polished concrete-effect porcelain.
