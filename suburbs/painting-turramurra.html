Painting in Turramurra | North Shore Projects

There is something distinctive about Turramurra's streetscapes. Dense canopies of mature gums and native trees shade the roads, creating a bushland feel that is rare this close to the city. The homes here reflect that character, many of them built decades ago with solid construction and generous proportions, tucked behind established gardens. Painting a home in Turramurra is not just about applying a fresh coat; it is about selecting colours and finishes that sit harmoniously within this unique natural setting. That is the approach North Shore Projects brings to every painting project in the suburb.

Colour Selection for Leafy Streetscapes

Choosing exterior colours in Turramurra requires a different sensibility than in more urban suburbs. The heavy tree cover casts dappled light and deep shade across facades, which means colours read differently here than they would in a sun-drenched coastal setting. We guide homeowners toward palettes that work with the surrounding landscape, favouring earthy tones, muted greens, warm greys and natural timber stains that complement rather than compete with the bushland backdrop. For homes visible from the street, we consider how the colour will appear through the filter of overhanging branches and seasonal foliage changes, ensuring the home looks inviting and well integrated into its environment year round.

Interior Modernisation of Older Homes

Many Turramurra homes were built in the 1950s through to the 1980s and, while structurally sound, their interiors often carry dated colour schemes, heavy feature walls and yellowed ceilings from decades past. A professional repaint is one of the most cost-effective ways to modernise these homes without the expense of a full renovation. We specialise in transforming dark, dated interiors into bright, contemporary living spaces using light, neutral tones that reflect natural light and create a sense of openness. Our team addresses every surface, from ceilings and cornices to door frames, skirting boards and built-in joinery, delivering a complete transformation that makes an older home feel new again. For helpful tips on this process, read our interior painting guide.

Character Home Painting with Care

Turramurra's character homes often feature original timber windows, ornate cornices, picture rails and decorative ceiling roses that deserve careful treatment. Our painters take the time to properly prepare these features, using fine sandpaper to smooth timber, caulking gaps where they have opened over time and applying primer to ensure paint adhesion on older surfaces. We use brushwork rather than rollers on detailed joinery and mouldings to achieve a crisp, even finish that highlights the craftsmanship of the original construction. The result is a home that feels refreshed and modern while retaining the character elements that give it personality.
