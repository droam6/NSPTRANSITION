Contemporary Tiling for Crows Nest Living

Crows Nest has established itself as one of the North Shore's most vibrant inner-city hubs, where a thriving cafe and dining scene sits alongside a diverse mix of housing that includes Victorian terraces, Federation cottages, Art Deco apartments and sleek modern developments. Residents here tend to have a strong sense of style and expect their homes to reflect the area's creative, contemporary energy. North Shore Projects is the tiling partner that Crows Nest locals turn to when they want results that match the suburb's design-forward character.

The demand for modern bathroom renovations in Crows Nest continues to grow as owners of older terraces and apartments seek to update wet areas that no longer meet current standards or aesthetic expectations. Many of the suburb's charming older buildings have bathrooms with original tilework that, while characterful, may be hiding waterproofing issues or simply look outdated alongside a newly renovated kitchen or living space. Our tilers strip back these spaces to the studs where necessary, resolve any moisture damage, apply compliant waterproofing and install new tiles that bring the bathroom firmly into the present.

Stylish Splashbacks and Feature Walls

Crows Nest homeowners are some of our most design-conscious clients, frequently requesting bold splashbacks, textured feature walls and statement tile choices that become the focal point of a room. We have installed everything from zellige tiles in jewel tones to matt black hexagonal mosaics in ensuite bathrooms, and from marble-look porcelain splashbacks to handmade ceramic tiles in open-plan kitchens. Our tilers have the precision and experience to execute intricate patterns and non-standard layouts, ensuring that your design vision translates perfectly from concept to finished surface. Apartment and Terrace Specialists

Working in Crows Nest means working within the constraints that come with terrace houses and apartment buildings. Limited access, shared walls, strata restrictions and narrow hallways are all part of the brief. Our team is experienced in managing logistics for tight spaces, carrying materials through narrow entries and stairwells, protecting common areas, and completing work within approved time frames. We coordinate closely with strata managers and body corporates to ensure every project runs smoothly and compliantly.
