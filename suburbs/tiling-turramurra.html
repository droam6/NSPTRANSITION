Bathroom Modernisation and Tiling for Turramurra Character Homes

Turramurra is one of the North Shore's quietest and most appealing residential suburbs, treasured for its leafy streetscapes, proximity to Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park and strong sense of community. The homes along Rohini Street, Ray Street and the streets branching off Eastern Road are predominantly established brick and weatherboard residences, many dating from the mid-twentieth century. While these character homes have wonderful bones, their bathrooms and kitchens often require modernisation to meet contemporary standards of comfort and functionality. North Shore Projects provides professional tiling services across Turramurra, bringing new life to these well-loved homes.

A typical Turramurra bathroom renovation begins with an honest assessment of the existing space. In many older homes, we encounter original ceramic tiles in dated colours, deteriorating grout and, in some cases, waterproofing that no longer meets current Australian Standards. Our tiling team carefully removes the old tiles, prepares the substrate and installs new waterproof membranes before laying your chosen tiles. We take a methodical approach that protects the integrity of the surrounding structure, which is especially important in timber-framed homes where moisture management is critical.

Bringing Turramurra Kitchens Up to Date

The kitchen is often the first room Turramurra homeowners choose to renovate when updating their home. A fresh splashback can completely change the character of the space without the cost of a full kitchen replacement. We install splashbacks in a wide variety of materials, from timeless white subway tiles to natural stone mosaics and the increasingly popular large-format porcelain panels that provide a seamless, grout-minimised finish. For inspiration on what works best in a North Shore kitchen, read our article on kitchen splashback trends.

Floor Tiling That Complements Character Features

Turramurra homes often feature beautiful original details such as timber flooring, decorative cornices and fireplaces. When introducing tiled floors to an entryway, bathroom or laundry, it is important to choose tiles that complement rather than compete with these features. Our team advises on tile formats, colours and grout selections that respect the character of your home. We are equally comfortable laying small-format mosaic tiles in a heritage-style bathroom as we are installing wide-plank timber-look porcelain in a modern extension.
