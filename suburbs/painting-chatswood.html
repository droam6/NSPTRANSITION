Painting in Chatswood | North Shore Projects

Chatswood is one of the most dynamic commercial and residential hubs on Sydney's North Shore, and the demand for professional painting services here reflects that energy. With its towering apartment complexes, bustling retail precincts and high-density living, painting in Chatswood requires a team that understands the unique challenges of working in compact, multi-storey environments. At North Shore Projects, we bring that expertise to every project we take on in this vibrant suburb.

High-Rise Apartment Painting Specialists

A significant portion of our work in Chatswood involves painting apartments within high-rise buildings. These projects demand careful coordination with building management, strata committees and body corporate regulations. We handle all the logistics, from securing lift access for materials to ensuring minimal disruption to neighbouring residents. Our painters are experienced in working within the tight spaces typical of Chatswood apartments, using low-odour, quick-drying paints that allow you to return to your living space the same day. We know how to maximise the sense of space in compact layouts through strategic colour selection, using lighter tones to open up rooms and accent walls to add depth without overwhelming smaller areas.

Commercial Painting for Retail and Office Spaces

The Chatswood CBD is home to major shopping centres, professional offices and a thriving restaurant scene. We provide commercial painting services tailored to the fast-paced nature of business fit-outs and retail refurbishments. Whether you need a complete office repaint before a new tenant moves in or a fresh colour scheme for your shopfront along Victoria Avenue, our team works after hours and on weekends to minimise downtime. We use durable, washable commercial-grade coatings that withstand heavy foot traffic and maintain a professional appearance for years.

Body Corporate and Strata Painting

Managing the painting of common areas across an entire apartment building is a complex undertaking. We regularly partner with body corporate managers and strata committees in Chatswood to deliver large-scale repainting of lobbies, hallways, stairwells, car parks and building exteriors. Our proposals include detailed colour consultations, comprehensive schedules that respect resident access needs, and competitive pricing structures suited to strata budgets. If your building is due for its cyclical repaint, we can provide a full condition assessment and staged painting plan that keeps costs manageable.
