Reliable Tiling Services in Artarmon

Artarmon is a quiet, well-established residential pocket on the lower North Shore that appeals to professionals, young couples and small families who value its proximity to Artarmon station and the short commute into the Sydney CBD. The suburb's housing ranges from compact two-bedroom units in low-rise blocks along Hampden Road and Jersey Road to detached homes on generous blocks in the streets closer to Willoughby. North Shore Projects has been providing tiling services to Artarmon residents for years, and we understand the practical approach that most homeowners here take to their renovation projects.

For many Artarmon residents, a tiling project is driven by necessity as much as aesthetics. Leaking showers, cracked floor tiles, outdated splashbacks and poorly waterproofed bathrooms are common issues in the suburb's older unit blocks and houses alike. Our licensed tilers address these problems at the source, stripping back damaged surfaces, repairing substrates, applying certified waterproofing membranes and installing new tiles that are built to perform. We do not cut corners, because the consequences of poor tiling work in a wet area can be costly and disruptive.

Unit Renovations and Strata Considerations

A significant number of our Artarmon projects involve apartment and unit renovations, and we are well-versed in the strata by-law requirements that govern this type of work. We can help you prepare the necessary documentation for your strata committee, ensure that waterproofing certificates are issued and retained, manage noise and access restrictions, and minimise disruption to neighbouring residents. Our team works efficiently within the time windows that strata buildings typically allow, completing bathroom retiles and kitchen upgrades on schedule and to the highest standard.

Kitchen and Bathroom Upgrades That Add Value

Whether you are planning to sell your Artarmon property or simply want to enjoy a more functional, modern space, updated tiling makes a measurable difference. In kitchens, a fresh splashback in a herringbone or subway pattern immediately lifts the room, while durable porcelain floor tiles are easy to maintain and look great for years. In bathrooms, we install everything from large-format wall tiles and frameless shower bases to mosaic feature strips and recessed niches. For help choosing the right tiles, read our guide on how to choose the perfect bathroom tiles.
