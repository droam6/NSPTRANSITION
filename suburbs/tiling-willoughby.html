Specialist Tiling for Willoughby Renovations

Willoughby is one of the North Shore's most family-friendly suburbs, known for its quiet, tree-lined streets and a housing stock that spans weatherboard cottages, solid brick bungalows and more recent townhouse developments. With Chatswood's shopping and transport hub just minutes away, Willoughby offers a quieter residential feel while remaining exceptionally well-connected. North Shore Projects has completed dozens of tiling projects throughout Willoughby, and we understand the unique requirements that come with renovating the suburb's older homes.

Many properties in Willoughby were built in the mid-twentieth century, and their bathrooms and kitchens often reflect the styles and materials of that era. Small mosaic floor tiles, pink or avocado fittings, and grout that has darkened over decades are common sights when we strip back an older Willoughby bathroom. Our tilers are experienced in working with these older structures, properly preparing substrates that may have settled or shifted over time, and ensuring waterproofing membranes are applied to current Australian Standards before a single new tile is laid.

Transforming Weatherboard and Brick Homes

The mix of weatherboard and brick construction in Willoughby means that wall and floor conditions vary from house to house. Weatherboard homes can present challenges with movement and moisture, which is why correct preparation is so critical. Our team assesses every surface before work begins, applying flexible adhesives and membranes where required to prevent cracking or water damage down the line. For brick homes, we ensure that rendered or plastered walls are sound and level before tiling, giving you a flawless, long-lasting finish that adds genuine value to your property.

Bathroom and Kitchen Upgrades That Last

Willoughby families tell us they want bathrooms and kitchens that look contemporary without being overly trendy, and that can stand up to the demands of a busy household. We regularly install hard-wearing porcelain floor tiles, easy-clean wall tiles in neutral tones, and practical yet stylish splashbacks that tie the kitchen together. If you are unsure which tiles are right for your space, our team can guide you through the selection process. For more ideas, take a look at our article on how to choose the perfect bathroom tiles.
