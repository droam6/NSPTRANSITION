Painting in Lane Cove | North Shore Projects

Expert Painters for Lane Cove Homes & Apartments

Lane Cove is one of Sydney's most picturesque lower North Shore suburbs, where leafy streets wind alongside the Lane Cove River and established family homes sit beside modern apartment complexes. At North Shore Projects, we provide professional painting services tailored to the unique character of Lane Cove properties. Whether you own a charming riverside cottage, a contemporary townhouse, or a unit within one of the many body corporate-managed buildings along Burns Bay Road, our licensed and insured painters deliver a finish that enhances both the beauty and the value of your home.

Riverside Homes & Family Residences

The riverside homes that line the streets of Lane Cove are among the most desirable on the North Shore. Many of these properties feature large outdoor areas, timber decking, and expansive facades that require specialist exterior painting. Our team understands the environmental demands placed on properties near the river, where humidity and moisture can accelerate paint deterioration. We use premium exterior coatings from Dulux and Taubmans that resist peeling, cracking, and mould growth, ensuring your home maintains its kerb appeal through every season. For family homes along Burns Bay Road and the surrounding streets, we offer full interior and exterior repaints that breathe new life into tired surfaces.

Body Corporate & Apartment Painting

Lane Cove has seen significant growth in apartment living over recent years, and our team is experienced in working with strata managers and body corporate committees to deliver large-scale painting projects on time and within budget. From common area refreshes to individual unit repaints, we coordinate efficiently to minimise disruption to residents. Our painters work cleanly and respectfully in shared spaces, and we provide detailed quoting so committees can make informed decisions about their building maintenance budgets.

Outdoor Deck Staining & Timber Finishes

Many Lane Cove homes feature beautiful timber decks and outdoor entertaining areas that take full advantage of the riverside setting. Over time, harsh UV rays and moisture cause timber to grey, split, and lose its natural warmth. Our deck staining and timber restoration service brings outdoor surfaces back to life with high-quality oil-based and water-based finishes that protect against the elements while showcasing the natural grain of the wood. We sand, clean, and prepare every surface before applying protective coatings that last for years.

We also offer complimentary colour consultation on every project, helping Lane Cove homeowners choose palettes that complement their architecture and surroundings. From soft neutrals that reflect the natural bushland to bold feature walls that make a statement, our team guides you through the selection process with confidence.
