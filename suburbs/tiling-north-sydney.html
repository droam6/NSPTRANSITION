Premium Tiling for North Sydney's Urban Landscape

North Sydney sits at the commercial heart of the lower North Shore, where gleaming office towers stand alongside modern residential high-rises and a scattering of heritage buildings that recall the suburb's earlier character. The area's dual identity as both a major business district and a sought-after residential address means that tiling work here spans a wide spectrum, from executive apartment bathrooms on the upper floors of Berry Street towers to reception areas and commercial washrooms in office buildings along the Pacific Highway. North Shore Projects brings the expertise and professionalism that North Sydney's demanding standards require.

Residential tiling in North Sydney overwhelmingly involves apartment renovations. Many of the suburb's high-rise buildings were constructed in the 1990s and 2000s, and their bathrooms and kitchens are now reaching the age where tiles are cracking, grout is discolouring and waterproofing membranes may be failing. Replacing tired tiles in a high-rise apartment requires careful logistics, from managing building access and lift bookings to protecting common areas and adhering to strata noise restrictions. Our team handles all of these details, allowing you to focus on selecting the tiles and finishes that will transform your space.

Modern Luxury Finishes

North Sydney apartment owners consistently gravitate toward clean, contemporary aesthetics with a luxury edge. Large-format rectified porcelain in tones of warm grey, white marble-look tiles with subtle veining, and matt black tapware set against pale, oversized wall tiles are all recurring themes in the projects we complete here. We specialise in achieving the razor-thin grout lines and perfectly level surfaces that these modern tile formats demand, using advanced levelling systems and precision cutting equipment. The result is a sophisticated, hotel-quality finish that elevates the entire apartment.

Commercial Tiling Services

Beyond residential work, North Shore Projects also provides commercial tiling for North Sydney's business premises. We have tiled office kitchen areas, building foyers, commercial bathrooms and retail fit-outs, working outside business hours where required to minimise disruption. Our commercial tiling work meets all relevant Australian Standards, and we provide detailed documentation including waterproofing certificates and compliance records.
