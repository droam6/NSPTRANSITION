Painting in Pymble | North Shore Projects

Pymble is home to some of the most impressive residential properties on Sydney's North Shore. Set on generous blocks with sweeping gardens, mature trees and often a swimming pool, these large estate homes present a scale of painting work that requires a team with the capacity, experience and organisational skills to deliver exceptional results across every room and every exterior surface. North Shore Projects has built a strong reputation among Pymble homeowners for managing exactly these kinds of substantial painting projects from start to finish.

Multi-Room Interior Painting Projects

When a large home needs repainting, it is rarely just one room. Pymble homeowners typically engage us to repaint entire wings of their home, or the full interior from bedrooms and bathrooms through to formal living rooms, home offices and entertainment spaces. We coordinate these multi-room projects with military precision, working room by room in a logical sequence that minimises disruption to your household. Furniture is carefully covered or moved, floors are protected with drop sheets, and each room is left clean and ready to use before we move on to the next. Our team can handle homes with ten or more rooms without compromising on the attention to detail that defines our work.

Exterior Painting for Weatherboard and Brick Homes

Pymble's residential landscape includes a mix of weatherboard, brick and rendered homes, each requiring a different approach to exterior painting. For weatherboard homes, we carry out comprehensive preparation including scraping back loose paint, sanding, filling gaps and caulking joints before applying a high-build primer and two coats of premium exterior acrylic. For face brick that needs refreshing, we offer limewash and paint finishes that breathe with the masonry while providing a clean, updated look. Rendered surfaces are inspected for cracks and repaired before being coated. No matter the substrate, our exterior painting process is thorough and designed to last.

Pool House and Outdoor Structure Painting

Many Pymble properties include a pool house, cabana, detached studio or garden shed that needs the same level of care as the main residence. These structures are exposed to constant moisture, chlorine vapour and direct sunlight, making paint selection critical. We use specialised exterior coatings that resist mould, mildew and UV degradation, ensuring your poolside structures maintain a fresh appearance season after season. We also paint pool fencing, pergolas and outdoor timber furniture to complete the look across your entire property.
