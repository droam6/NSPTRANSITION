Painting in St Ives | North Shore Projects

St Ives occupies a unique position on the North Shore, where suburban living meets the bushland fringes of Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park. The suburb has seen significant development in recent years, with new architect-designed homes joining the established residences that have long defined its wide, quiet streets. Whether you have just completed a new build that needs its first coat of paint or you are refreshing a home that has been in the family for decades, North Shore Projects provides painting services in St Ives that match the scale, quality and ambition of your property.

New Build Painting to the Highest Standard

The final paint finish on a new home is one of the most visible indicators of build quality, and getting it right is essential. We work closely with builders, project managers and homeowners in St Ives to deliver new build painting that meets the exacting standards expected of premium construction. Our process includes a thorough inspection of all plastered surfaces for imperfections, careful masking and protection of installed fixtures, and a systematic application of primer, undercoat and topcoats using spray, roller and brush techniques appropriate to each surface. We understand the sequencing required on a construction site and coordinate with other trades to ensure painting is completed on schedule without compromising quality.

Contemporary Colour Palettes for Open-Plan Living

St Ives' newer homes are often designed around expansive open-plan layouts with high ceilings, floor-to-ceiling glazing and seamless indoor-outdoor connections. These spaces call for colour palettes that enhance the sense of light and volume while creating warmth and intimacy. We guide St Ives homeowners toward sophisticated neutral palettes, think warm whites, soft greiges, and subtle stone tones, that amplify natural light and provide a versatile backdrop for furnishings. Where clients want to make a bolder statement, we introduce depth through darker tones on feature walls, joinery or ceiling planes, creating visual anchors within the open layout. Our interior painting tips offer more ideas on colour strategy for modern living spaces.

Exterior Painting for Rendered Homes

Rendered exteriors are the dominant cladding type across St Ives, from smooth acrylic render to textured cement render and modern bagged brick finishes. Each render type requires specific preparation and paint systems to achieve a lasting result. We inspect rendered surfaces for hairline cracks, efflorescence and areas of moisture ingress before commencing any painting. Cracks are opened, filled and sealed with flexible fillers that move with the substrate, preventing recurrence. We then apply elastomeric or membrane paints designed for rendered surfaces, providing a waterproof, breathable and UV-resistant coating that keeps your home looking sharp through years of sun, wind and rain.
