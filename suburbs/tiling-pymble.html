Tiling in Pymble | North Shore Projects

Expert Tiling for Pymble's Spacious Family Homes

Pymble is synonymous with generous family living on the upper North Shore. Set among leafy streets and bushland reserves, the suburb is home to some of the area's largest residential properties, with many sitting on blocks of 1,000 square metres or more. Streets such as Grandview Drive, Telegraph Road and Pymble Avenue are lined with substantial homes that often feature multiple bathrooms, expansive kitchen areas and covered outdoor entertaining spaces, all of which benefit enormously from professional tiling. North Shore Projects is the trusted local choice for Pymble tiling services, delivering premium results on projects of every scale.

Many Pymble families are investing in home renovations rather than upsizing, choosing to modernise their existing kitchens and bathrooms to suit contemporary living. A full bathroom retile in a typical Pymble home involves stripping back the old surfaces, repairing or replacing the substrate, installing a compliant waterproof membrane and laying new tiles with precision. Our team manages this entire process, working efficiently to minimise disruption to your household. We are experienced with the specific challenges of renovating in established properties, including managing asbestos-era materials where necessary and working around existing plumbing configurations.

Outdoor Tiling and Entertaining Areas

One of the distinctive features of Pymble homes is the emphasis on outdoor living. Large covered patios, alfresco dining areas and poolside entertaining zones are common throughout the suburb. Tiling these outdoor spaces requires specialist knowledge of drainage gradients, slip-resistant surfaces and adhesives that withstand Sydney's weather extremes. Our Pymble tiling team installs outdoor porcelain pavers, natural stone and textured tiles that look stunning while meeting Australian safety standards for wet areas. Pool coping tiles and surrounding decking areas are a particular specialty, and we ensure every outdoor installation is built to last through years of sun, rain and chlorine exposure.

Bathroom and Kitchen Transformations

Inside the home, we handle everything from compact powder room refreshes to complete master bathroom overhauls. Pymble homeowners often favour premium materials including honed marble, limestone and oversized porcelain slabs that create a luxurious, hotel-like atmosphere. In kitchens, a new splashback can completely transform the look and feel of the space.
