Character Home Cleaning and Weekly Services in Turramurra

Turramurra is a suburb defined by its beautiful character homes, towering eucalyptus trees and a strong sense of community. Nestled between Pymble and Wahroonga on Sydney's upper North Shore, Turramurra's residential streets are home to a wonderful mix of Federation-era houses, inter-war bungalows and mid-century properties, many of which have been lovingly maintained or carefully extended over the decades. These character homes feature intricate period details that require a cleaning team with the knowledge and care to look after them properly. North Shore Projects provides expert cleaning services in Turramurra that respect the unique features of these homes while delivering the immaculate results that residents expect.

Our cleaners are trained to work around the ornate timber joinery, decorative ceiling roses, leadlight windows, polished hardwood floors and original fireplaces that are so common in Turramurra homes. We understand that these features cannot be treated the same way as modern surfaces, and we select our cleaning products and techniques accordingly. Gentle, pH-neutral solutions protect delicate timberwork, while microfibre cloths and soft brushes ensure that intricate mouldings and cornices are dusted without damage.

Regular Weekly Cleaning for Busy Families

Turramurra is a popular choice for families with school-age children, drawn by its proximity to excellent schools and its safe, community-oriented atmosphere. For these busy households, our weekly cleaning service is an invaluable time-saver. Each visit covers the essentials: vacuuming and mopping all floors, cleaning and sanitising bathrooms, wiping down kitchen surfaces and appliances, dusting throughout, making beds and general tidying. We build a relationship with each family over time, learning their preferences and ensuring a consistent standard week after week. Our team arrives on the same day at the same time, so you always know when to expect us.

Window Cleaning Services

Turramurra homes are surrounded by lush gardens and mature trees, which means windows can accumulate pollen, dust and water spots surprisingly quickly. Our window cleaning service restores clarity and sparkle to every pane, both inside and out. We clean window frames and tracks, remove cobwebs from surrounds, and leave glass streak-free using professional-grade squeegees and purified water techniques. For homes with period-style timber-framed windows, we take extra care to avoid moisture damage to the frames. Regular window cleaning is a popular add-on to our weekly or fortnightly domestic cleaning package, and many Turramurra clients book a comprehensive window clean at least twice a year.
