Luxury Tiling for Mosman's Finest Homes

Mosman is one of Sydney's most prestigious residential suburbs, where grand harbourside homes, heritage-listed properties and architect-designed residences line streets that wind between Balmoral Beach, Clifton Gardens and Taronga Zoo. The calibre of housing in Mosman demands tiling work of an equally high standard, and North Shore Projects has earned the trust of discerning Mosman homeowners by consistently delivering results that meet the exacting expectations of this remarkable suburb. When the stakes are high and the materials are premium, our team rises to the occasion every time.

Tiling projects in Mosman are often part of larger, architect-led renovations or new builds where no detail is too small to warrant careful consideration. We work collaboratively with interior designers, architects and project managers to execute tiling specifications with absolute precision. Whether the brief calls for Italian Carrara marble in a master ensuite, hand-glazed Moroccan zellige tiles in a powder room, or oversized porcelain slabs in a pool house, our tilers have the skills and experience to handle premium materials with the care they deserve.

Imported Materials and Designer Bathrooms

Mosman homeowners regularly source tiles from specialist importers and boutique suppliers, selecting materials that are unavailable through standard retail channels. Working with imported tiles requires a heightened level of care, since replacement stock may not be readily available and the tiles themselves can be more delicate or irregular than mass-produced alternatives. Our tilers are experienced in handling natural stone, handmade ceramics, glass mosaics and bespoke porcelain, adjusting adhesive selection, cutting techniques and installation methods to suit each material's unique characteristics. The result is a finish that does justice to both the tile and the space it occupies. For inspiration on selecting the right tiles, explore our guide on kitchen splashback trends for 2026.

Heritage and Period-Appropriate Tiling

Many Mosman properties carry heritage overlays or conservation area requirements that influence the scope and style of any renovation work. Our team understands the importance of sympathetic tiling choices in these contexts, sourcing period-appropriate tiles that satisfy heritage guidelines while delivering the performance and durability that modern life requires. From tessellated verandah tiles to encaustic bathroom floors and classic white subway walls, we install tilework that respects the original character of Mosman's most treasured homes.
