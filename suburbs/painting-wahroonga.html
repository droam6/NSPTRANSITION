Painting in Wahroonga | North Shore Projects

Wahroonga is the crown jewel of Sydney's upper North Shore, a suburb synonymous with grand residences, sweeping driveways and some of the most architecturally significant homes in the region. From stately federation mansions set behind established hedgerows to elegant inter-war homes with imposing facades, the properties here demand a painting service that operates at the highest level. North Shore Projects has earned the trust of Wahroonga homeowners by consistently delivering the premium quality, meticulous attention to detail and refined aesthetic judgement that these exceptional homes require.

Grand Homes and Mansion Painting

Painting a grand Wahroonga home is a substantial undertaking that goes far beyond what a typical residential paint job involves. These properties often encompass twenty or more rooms, multiple levels, extensive hallways, formal entertaining spaces and service areas, each requiring its own consideration of colour, finish and durability. We approach these large-scale projects with careful planning, beginning with a comprehensive walk-through to document every surface, discuss your vision for each space and develop a room-by-room specification. Our team is large enough to handle the scale of these projects while maintaining the consistency and precision that a home of this calibre demands. We work methodically through the property, ensuring each room is completed to an impeccable standard before moving on.

Premium Paint Brands and Heritage Colour Schemes

Wahroonga's finest homes deserve the finest materials. We exclusively use premium paint brands including Dulux, Haymes and Porter's Paints, selecting products that offer superior coverage, depth of colour and longevity. For heritage properties, we draw on specialised heritage colour ranges that have been carefully researched to reflect the palettes of specific architectural periods. Whether your home calls for the rich, saturated tones of the federation era, the elegant neutrals of an inter-war Arts and Crafts residence, or a contemporary reinterpretation of classic colours, we match the paint system to the architecture. Our colour consultations are thorough, involving on-site sampling under natural light conditions to ensure the chosen palette looks exactly right on your home. For more on selecting the perfect scheme, see our guide to house repainting.

High Ceilings and Detailed Trim Work

Many of Wahroonga's grand homes feature soaring ceilings, elaborate cornices, ceiling roses, picture rails, dado rails and detailed architraves that require skilled brushwork and considerable patience. Painting high ceilings safely and effectively requires proper scaffolding, not just ladders, and our team is fully equipped and trained to work at height within residential interiors. We use extension platforms and indoor scaffolding systems that protect floors and furniture while providing stable, safe access to every surface. For the intricate trim work that defines these homes, our painters use fine brushes and a steady hand to cut in around mouldings, achieve crisp lines where walls meet ornate cornices and deliver a finish that highlights rather than obscures the craftsmanship of the original joinery.

From a single formal room to a complete mansion repaint, our Wahroonga painting team brings the expertise, premium materials and unwavering standards that your home deserves.
