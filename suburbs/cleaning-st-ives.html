Cleaning in St Ives | North Shore Projects

Modern Home Cleaning and Post-Construction Services in St Ives

St Ives is one of the North Shore's most desirable residential suburbs, renowned for its spacious blocks, bush-bordered streets and a steady stream of impressive new builds and major renovations. The suburb attracts families who appreciate the space and natural beauty of the area, and many St Ives properties are large, modern homes featuring open-plan living, expansive kitchens with stone benchtops, multiple bathrooms with floor-to-ceiling tiles, and seamless indoor-outdoor entertaining areas. These contemporary homes require a professional cleaning team that understands how to care for high-end finishes and maintain the polished look that St Ives homeowners expect. North Shore Projects provides exactly that.

With so much new construction activity in St Ives, post-construction cleaning is one of our most frequently requested services in the area. Building a new home or completing a major renovation is an exciting milestone, but the final stages of construction leave behind a considerable mess: fine plaster dust on every surface, paint splatters on windows and frames, cement residue on tiles, sawdust in cupboards, adhesive marks on glass and a general film of building grime throughout the property. Our post-construction cleaning team is equipped with the specialised tools and products needed to safely and effectively remove all traces of building work without damaging newly installed surfaces.

Post-Construction Cleaning for New Builds

Our post-construction cleaning service for St Ives new builds is comprehensive and methodical. We work room by room, starting from the top of the house and working down. We remove protective plastic and tape, clean all windows inside and out, wipe down every surface including benchtops, cabinetry and shelving, vacuum and mop all floors, clean bathroom fixtures and fittings, remove labels and stickers from appliances, and perform a final detailed inspection to ensure the home is move-in ready. For many St Ives families, this is the exciting final step before they can move into their brand-new home and enjoy it for the first time.

Regular Cleaning for New and Established Homes

Once you are settled in, our regular domestic cleaning service ensures your St Ives home stays in top condition. We offer weekly, fortnightly and monthly packages that cover all living areas, bedrooms, bathrooms and the kitchen. Our cleaners are experienced with the modern finishes common in St Ives homes, including engineered stone benchtops, matte-finish cabinetry, large-format floor tiles and frameless glass shower screens. We use the appropriate products for each surface to maintain their appearance and longevity.

Deep Kitchen and Bathroom Cleaning

The kitchens and bathrooms in St Ives homes are often the most impressive rooms in the house, and keeping them in pristine condition requires regular professional attention. Our deep kitchen cleaning service targets grease buildup on rangehoods, oven interiors, stovetops and splashbacks, while our bathroom deep clean focuses on descaling taps and shower screens, removing soap scum and mould, cleaning grout lines and restoring surfaces to their original shine. Many St Ives clients add a quarterly deep clean to their regular cleaning schedule for the best results.
