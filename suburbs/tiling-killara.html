Tiling in Killara | North Shore Projects

High-End Tiling for Killara's Prestigious Homes

Killara is one of the North Shore's most sought-after residential suburbs, renowned for its wide, tree-lined streets, sprawling properties and beautifully preserved heritage architecture. Homes along Springdale Road, Locksley Street and the streets surrounding Killara Park are among Sydney's finest, and their owners expect nothing less than exceptional craftsmanship when it comes to renovation work. North Shore Projects provides premium tiling services in Killara, bringing the precision and attention to detail that these distinguished homes deserve.

Many properties in Killara were built during the Federation and Inter-War periods, and their bathrooms and kitchens often require a sensitive approach to modernisation. Original mosaic floors, patterned borders and period-appropriate tile selections are considerations that a standard tiling contractor may overlook. Our team has extensive experience working with Killara homeowners to select tiles that complement the character of their property while introducing modern functionality. Whether it is restoring a traditional tessellated verandah floor or installing a luxurious freestanding bath surround with natural marble tiles, we treat every Killara project with the care it warrants.

Luxury Bathroom Renovations in Killara

Killara bathrooms often feature generous proportions that allow for creative tile layouts and premium materials. We regularly install large-format porcelain slabs, natural stone such as Carrara marble and travertine, and handmade artisan tiles for feature walls. Proper waterproofing is critical in these older homes, where original structures may need additional membrane treatment before tiling can commence. We manage every step from demolition through to final grout sealing. For guidance on selecting the ideal tiles for your renovation, explore our article on how to choose bathroom tiles.

Kitchen Splashbacks and Floor Tiling

Killara kitchens are often the centrepiece of the home, designed for both serious cooking and entertaining. A beautifully tiled splashback can elevate the entire space, and we offer installation in a wide range of materials including glass, ceramic subway tiles and stone mosaics. For open-plan living and dining areas, we lay floor tiles that transition seamlessly from indoor spaces to covered outdoor areas, which are a hallmark of Killara's generous family homes. Check out our piece on kitchen splashback trends for the latest design ideas.
