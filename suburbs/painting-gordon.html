Painting in Gordon | North Shore Projects

Gordon sits at the heart of the upper North Shore, a suburb defined by established family homes, leafy cul-de-sacs and a strong sense of community centred around Gordon station and its surrounding village shops. For families living here, keeping the home looking fresh and well maintained is a priority, and that is exactly where North Shore Projects comes in. We provide comprehensive painting services designed specifically for the family homes that make Gordon such a sought-after place to live.

Interior Painting for Busy Family Homes

Family life takes its toll on interior walls. Scuff marks in hallways, crayon drawings in the kids' rooms, faded paint in high-traffic areas and outdated colour schemes that no longer suit the way you use your home are all common reasons Gordon homeowners call us. We approach every interior repaint with a focus on durability and practicality, recommending washable, low-sheen finishes for living areas and hallways that can handle the daily demands of family life. For children's bedrooms, we use low-VOC paints that are safe for young families, and we work efficiently to minimise disruption, typically completing a standard room in a single day so your household routine stays on track.

Living Area and Open-Plan Refreshes

Many of Gordon's homes were built or renovated with open-plan living in mind, where the kitchen, dining and lounge areas flow together as one connected space. Painting these rooms requires a cohesive colour strategy that ties the zones together while allowing each area to feel distinct. We work with homeowners to select complementary palettes, using subtle shifts in tone or a well-placed feature wall to define spaces without breaking the visual flow. The result is a living area that feels unified, modern and inviting for the whole family.

Exterior Repainting for Established Homes

Gordon's streetscapes are lined with homes ranging from 1960s brick residences to more recent architecturally designed builds. Over time, exterior paint fades, timber trims deteriorate and rendered surfaces develop hairline cracks that allow moisture in. Our exterior painting service begins with a thorough inspection and preparation phase, including pressure washing, scraping, sanding, crack filling and priming. We then apply premium exterior paints that are formulated to withstand Sydney's harsh UV exposure and seasonal rain. For guidance on when your home might be due for an exterior refresh, our repainting schedule guide is a helpful resource.

Whether you need a single room painted or a full interior and exterior transformation, our Gordon painting team delivers a finish that adds lasting value to your family home.

  • Kids' room painting and nursery-safe finishes
  • Open-plan living area colour consultations
  • Full exterior repainting and timber maintenance
  • Licensed, insured and 5-star rated on Google
  • Free on-site quotes with no obligation
