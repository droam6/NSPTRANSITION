Tiling in Roseville | North Shore Projects

Expert Tiling for Roseville's Well-Maintained Homes

Roseville sits in a prized position on the North Shore line, offering residents a peaceful, tree-lined environment within easy reach of both Chatswood's amenities to the south and the upper North Shore suburbs to the north. The streets around Roseville Park, including Lord Street, Maclaurin Parade and Bancroft Avenue, are home to some of the area's most attractive and well-cared-for properties. Roseville homeowners take pride in their homes, and when the time comes for a bathroom refresh, a new kitchen splashback or updated floor tiling, they look for a tradesperson who shares that commitment to quality. North Shore Projects delivers exactly that level of care in every Roseville tiling project we undertake.

The housing stock in Roseville spans several eras, from elegant Federation homes with original tessellated tile verandahs through to mid-century brick residences and recent townhouse developments closer to the station. Each property type presents its own tiling considerations. In older homes, we often encounter uneven substrates, outdated waterproofing and original tiles that require careful removal to avoid damaging surrounding plasterwork. Our experienced tilers handle these challenges daily, ensuring a smooth transition from old to new without compromising the structural integrity of the home.

Bathroom and Laundry Tiling Specialists

Bathrooms and laundries are the most common tiling projects in Roseville. A full bathroom retile typically involves removing existing wall and floor tiles, repairing or replacing cement sheeting as needed, applying a compliant waterproof membrane and then installing new tiles with precision-cut edges and consistent grout joints. We work with a wide range of tile sizes and materials, from classic ceramic wall tiles to large-format porcelain floor tiles that create a clean, expansive look. Laundry rooms benefit from hardwearing, easy-to-clean tiles on both walls and floors, and we can recommend slip-resistant options for wet zones. For detailed guidance, check out our article on how to choose bathroom tiles.

Kitchen Splashbacks and Feature Walls

Roseville kitchens often feature quality cabinetry and stone benchtops, and a well-installed splashback brings the entire design together. We tile splashbacks in every style, from stacked subway tiles in soft neutrals to bold geometric patterns and seamless porcelain panels that eliminate grout lines almost entirely. Feature walls in bathrooms and living areas are another growing trend in Roseville homes, and our team creates striking accent installations using textured tiles, natural stone and decorative mosaics.
