Painting in Killara | North Shore Projects

Killara is renowned for its tree-lined streets and some of the finest heritage homes on the North Shore. The suburb's federation-era residences, with their ornate detailing, decorative fretwork and distinctive timber features, deserve painters who appreciate the craftsmanship that went into building them. At North Shore Projects, we specialise in heritage home painting in Killara, treating every period detail with the care and respect it demands.

Federation Colour Palettes and Period-Appropriate Finishes

Choosing the right colours for a heritage property is about more than personal taste. It involves understanding the architectural era, the original colour conventions and the council guidelines that often apply in heritage conservation areas like parts of Killara. Our painters are well versed in federation colour palettes, drawing from traditional combinations of deep reds, heritage greens, creams and ochres that honour the character of your home. We consult closely with homeowners to find a palette that respects the period while meeting your personal style preferences, ensuring the finished result looks authentic and beautifully maintained.

Careful Preparation of Ornate Features

The difference between an average paint job and a truly exceptional one on a heritage home comes down to preparation. In Killara, many homes feature elaborate timber mouldings, decorative eaves brackets, turned veranda posts and intricate fretwork panels. We take the time to carefully sand, fill and prime each element before any topcoat is applied. Where timber has weathered or deteriorated, we carry out minor carpentry repairs to restore profiles before painting. This meticulous approach ensures crisp paint lines, a smooth finish and long-lasting protection against the elements. For more on getting preparation right, see our guide to house repainting schedules.

Exterior Timber Painting and Protection

Killara's established homes often feature extensive exterior timber, from weatherboard cladding and window frames to veranda decking and fascia boards. Our team uses premium acrylic and oil-based paints specifically formulated for Australian conditions, providing superior adhesion, flexibility and UV resistance. We pay particular attention to areas prone to moisture exposure, ensuring proper sealing and priming to prevent peeling, cracking and timber rot. The goal is always a finish that looks magnificent and protects your investment for years to come.

Whether you are restoring a grand federation home to its former glory or simply refreshing the interior of your Killara property, our licensed painters deliver results that consistently exceed expectations.
