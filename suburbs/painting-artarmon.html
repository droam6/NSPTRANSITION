Painting Services for Artarmon Apartments & Homes

Artarmon sits at a unique crossroads on the lower North Shore, where a compact residential neighbourhood meets one of Sydney's busiest commercial and industrial precincts. The suburb is home to a mix of older brick homes, modern apartment complexes, and an increasing number of renovated properties that reflect the area's growing popularity with young professionals and families. At North Shore Projects, we offer painting services designed to meet the specific needs of Artarmon property owners, whether you are refreshing your own home, preparing a rental for new tenants, or upgrading a commercial space near the Artarmon industrial area.

Apartment Repaints & Strata Work

Artarmon's apartment market continues to grow, and keeping units looking fresh and modern is essential for both owner-occupiers and investors. Our team specialises in apartment repaints that transform tired interiors into bright, contemporary living spaces. We work efficiently within the confines of apartment buildings, respecting strata by-laws, coordinating with building managers, and ensuring minimal disruption to neighbours. Whether it is a single-bedroom unit or a larger apartment, we prepare every surface meticulously and apply premium low-VOC paints that deliver a smooth, professional finish. We also handle common area painting for strata committees looking to improve their building's presentation.

Fresh Modern Interiors for Rental Properties

For Artarmon landlords and property managers, a professionally painted interior is one of the most effective ways to attract quality tenants and maximise rental returns. Our landlord painting service is designed for efficiency and value. We use durable, washable paint finishes in neutral palettes that appeal to the broadest range of tenants while standing up to the wear and tear of rental living. We can turn around vacant property repaints quickly, minimising the time your investment sits empty between tenancies. Our team handles everything from patching nail holes and repairing minor wall damage to full interior repaints that make properties look brand new.

We understand the needs of investment property owners in Artarmon. Our landlord painting packages are competitively priced and include all preparation, materials, and labour.
