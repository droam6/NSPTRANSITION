Spring Cleaning for Killara Homes

With the changing seasons, Killara homes benefit enormously from a thorough spring clean. Our spring cleaning service goes beyond the regular routine to address areas that accumulate dust and grime over the cooler months. We clean behind furniture, wash curtains and blinds, dust ceiling fans and light fittings, deep clean carpets and upholstery, and pay special attention to the detailed timber joinery and decorative mouldings that give Killara homes their distinctive character. It is the ideal way to refresh your home and welcome the warmer weather.

Post-Renovation Cleaning After Heritage Restorations

Many Killara homeowners invest in careful restorations to preserve and enhance the heritage features of their properties. Whether you have just completed a period-sympathetic bathroom renovation, restored original timber floors, or extended a Federation home, the construction process inevitably leaves behind a layer of fine dust, plaster residue and debris. Our post-renovation cleaning service is designed to thoroughly remove all traces of building work, including detailed cleaning of newly installed surfaces, removal of paint overspray from glass, and careful dusting of every cornice, architrave and skirting board.
