Family Home Cleaning and End-of-Lease Services in Gordon

Gordon sits at the heart of Sydney's upper North Shore, a suburb loved by families for its excellent schools, leafy residential streets and convenient access to the Pacific Highway and Gordon Station. With a mix of established family homes and a growing number of rental apartments near the station precinct, Gordon has a diverse range of cleaning needs that North Shore Projects is perfectly equipped to handle. Whether you are a busy family needing regular house cleaning or a tenant preparing to vacate a rental unit, our professional team delivers consistent, high-quality results every time.

Regular domestic cleaning is our bread and butter in Gordon. Many families in the suburb juggle demanding work schedules with school drop-offs, weekend sport and social commitments, leaving little time for thorough housework. Our regular cleaning service takes that burden away entirely. We work to a detailed checklist that covers all living areas, bedrooms, bathrooms and the kitchen, and we are happy to accommodate specific requests such as ironing, laundry folding or fridge cleaning. You can choose weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits to suit your family's routine.

End-of-Lease Cleaning Near Gordon Station

The apartment complexes and townhouses clustered around Gordon Station and along Pacific Highway see regular tenant turnover, making end-of-lease cleaning one of our most popular services in the area. Our end-of-lease package is comprehensive and designed to meet the exacting standards of North Shore property managers. We clean every room from top to bottom, including oven interiors, rangehood filters, window tracks, light fittings, built-in wardrobes and all bathroom fixtures. We offer a bond-back guarantee, and if anything is flagged during the final inspection, we return to rectify it at no extra charge. Check our end of lease cleaning checklist for a detailed overview of what is included.

Oven and Kitchen Deep Cleans

Gordon families who love to cook know how quickly ovens, stovetops and rangehoods accumulate baked-on grease and carbon buildup. Our dedicated oven and kitchen deep cleaning service targets these hard-to-clean areas using commercial-grade degreasers and specialised techniques. We disassemble removable oven components, soak and scrub grates, clean oven glass inside and out, degrease rangehood filters and restore stovetops to a like-new condition. It is a popular add-on to our regular cleaning service and an essential component of every end-of-lease clean.

School Holiday Deep Cleans

The school holidays are the perfect time to give your Gordon home a thorough deep clean. While the kids are away at camp or visiting grandparents, our team can work through the entire house without disruption, tackling the areas that are often overlooked during the regular routine. We deep clean carpets, wash windows, dust blinds and ceiling fans, sanitise mattresses and clean behind and under all furniture. It is a fantastic way to reset your home and start the new term with a fresh, clean living environment.
