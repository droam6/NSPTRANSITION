Elegant Tiling Solutions for Neutral Bay Properties

Neutral Bay occupies a coveted stretch of the lower North Shore between the bustle of North Sydney's commercial district and the calm harbour frontage of Cremorne and Mosman. The suburb is home to a refined mix of elegant Art Deco apartment buildings, well-maintained inter-war homes and contemporary residential developments, many commanding views across Sydney Harbour. Residents here have discerning taste, and when it comes to tiling, they expect craftsmanship that honours the character of their properties while delivering a polished, enduring finish. North Shore Projects has built a strong reputation among Neutral Bay homeowners for doing exactly that.

The tiling projects we undertake in Neutral Bay often centre on premium bathroom and kitchen renovations. Owners of the suburb's elegant older apartments frequently come to us with bathrooms that retain their original 1930s or 1940s tilework. While these spaces have charm, they often lack the waterproofing integrity and functional layout that modern living demands. Our approach is to carefully strip back these spaces, remedy any underlying moisture issues, install compliant waterproofing membranes and lay new tiles that complement the apartment's architectural period while bringing the wet area up to current standards.

Premium Bathroom Renovations

Neutral Bay bathrooms call for a refined material palette. We frequently work with Calacatta marble-look porcelain, honed limestone tiles, large-format white-on-white textures and soft neutral tones that reflect the suburb's coastal elegance. Feature walls with book-matched porcelain slabs, frameless shower recesses with precision-mitred tile edges and custom mosaic shower floors are all part of our repertoire. Every installation is backed by certified waterproofing, ensuring your investment is protected for years to come. For guidance on selecting tiles that suit your space, read our article on how to choose the perfect bathroom tiles.

Kitchen Renovations with Harbour-Side Style

A new kitchen splashback can completely redefine the feel of a Neutral Bay kitchen, especially in open-plan apartments where the kitchen flows directly into living and dining areas with harbour views. We install splashbacks in materials ranging from classic subway tiles and natural stone to full-height porcelain slabs that create a seamless, uninterrupted surface. Our precise cutting around power points, rangehoods and window reveals ensures a clean, professional result that integrates perfectly with your cabinetry and benchtops.
