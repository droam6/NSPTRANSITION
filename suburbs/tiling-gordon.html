Tiling in Gordon | North Shore Projects

Trusted Tiling Services for Gordon Homes and Apartments

Gordon sits at the heart of the upper North Shore, centred around its busy train station and the popular Gordon Centre shopping precinct. The suburb offers a diverse mix of housing, from established family homes on generous blocks along streets like Rosedale Road and Werona Avenue, through to modern apartment complexes clustered near the station along Pacific Highway and St Johns Avenue. This variety means that Gordon homeowners have a wide range of tiling needs, and North Shore Projects is equipped to handle every one of them.

Families upgrading their Gordon home frequently start with the bathroom or kitchen, two spaces where quality tiling makes the biggest visual and functional difference. A dated bathroom with chipped tiles and discoloured grout not only looks tired but can also harbour mould and compromise waterproofing. Our Gordon tiling team strips back existing surfaces, assesses the substrate, applies Australian Standard-compliant waterproof membranes and installs new tiles with meticulous precision. The result is a bathroom that looks beautiful, functions flawlessly and adds genuine value to your property.

Kitchen and Splashback Tiling in Gordon

Gordon kitchens often serve as the social hub of the household, particularly in the family homes that line the quieter streets east of the railway line toward St Ives. A well-chosen splashback ties together benchtops, cabinetry and appliances, and our tilers install everything from classic white subway tiles to contemporary textured ceramics and stone-look porcelain. If you are weighing up options, our blog post on kitchen splashback trends covers the most popular choices for North Shore kitchens right now.

Floor Tiling for Every Room

Whether you need hardwearing porcelain for a high-traffic hallway, slip-resistant tiles for a laundry or elegant natural stone for an entryway, our Gordon tiling team delivers flawless floor installations. We take particular care with tile layout and levelling, ensuring consistent grout lines and perfectly flat surfaces even in older Gordon homes where subfloors may have settled over the decades. For open-plan renovations, we can lay continuous floor tiles from the kitchen through the living area and out to the alfresco, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow.
