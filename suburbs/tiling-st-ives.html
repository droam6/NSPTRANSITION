Tiling in St Ives | North Shore Projects

Tiling for New Builds, Renovations and Outdoor Living in St Ives

St Ives is one of the North Shore's most spacious and family-oriented suburbs, known for its large residential blocks, proximity to bushland and an active building scene that sees both new homes rising and established properties undergoing significant renovations. Located east of the Pacific Highway and bordered by Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park, St Ives offers a semi-rural feel that is rare this close to Sydney's CBD. Streets such as Horace Street, Woodbury Road and Memorial Avenue feature homes on generous allotments that often include multiple living zones, several bathrooms and substantial outdoor entertaining areas. North Shore Projects provides comprehensive tiling services across St Ives, handling everything from a single bathroom retile through to multi-room installations for new constructions.

The new build market in St Ives is particularly active, with older homes on large blocks being demolished and replaced with contemporary two-storey residences designed for modern family life. These new builds typically require tiling across multiple bathrooms, a large kitchen splashback, floor tiling throughout living areas and often extensive outdoor tiling as well. Our team works alongside builders and project managers on new constructions, coordinating our schedule with other trades to ensure the tiling phase is completed on time and to the highest standard. We are experienced with the full range of tile formats used in contemporary St Ives homes, from oversized 1200x600mm porcelain slabs to small hexagonal mosaics for shower niches and feature strips.

Pool Surrounds and Outdoor Entertaining

With larger-than-average blocks and a lifestyle centred around outdoor living, St Ives homes frequently incorporate pools, covered patios and barbecue areas that require professional tiling. Pool coping, surrounding paving and outdoor kitchen surfaces all demand tiles that are slip-resistant, frost-resistant and able to handle the harsh Australian sun without fading or cracking. Our tilers select and install products specifically rated for external use, and we pay careful attention to drainage falls so that water flows away from the house and pool rather than pooling on the surface. The result is an outdoor space that is as functional as it is beautiful.

Bathroom Renovations for Established St Ives Homes

Not every project in St Ives involves a new build. Many homeowners in the suburb are choosing to renovate their existing bathrooms and kitchens rather than rebuild from scratch. A full bathroom renovation in an established St Ives home typically involves removing dated tiles, repairing or replacing the substrate, installing new waterproofing and tiling with modern materials that completely refresh the space.
