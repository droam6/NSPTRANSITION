Cleaning in Neutral Bay | North Shore Projects

Premium Cleaning for Neutral Bay Waterfront Apartments and Homes

Neutral Bay occupies one of the most desirable positions on Sydney's lower North Shore, with many of its apartment buildings and homes offering stunning views across Sydney Harbour, the Bridge and the city skyline. The suburb stretches from the bustling Military Road retail strip down to the waterfront at Hayes Street Wharf and Kurraba Point, encompassing a mix of art deco apartment blocks, modern high-rises and elegant period homes. North Shore Projects delivers premium cleaning services that match the quality of Neutral Bay living, with particular expertise in maintaining waterfront and harbour-view properties where presentation standards are exceptionally high.

When you live in a harbour-view apartment, clean windows are not a luxury but a necessity. Salt spray, dust, pollen and moisture from the harbour environment can quickly dull glass and leave streaks that obscure the views you are paying a premium to enjoy. Our cleaning team uses professional-grade, streak-free window cleaning techniques that leave your glass crystal clear, allowing natural light to flood in and your harbour panorama to look its absolute best. We clean both internal and accessible external glass, along with window tracks, frames and sills that accumulate grime from the maritime environment.

Regular Domestic Cleaning

Many Neutral Bay residents lead busy professional lives, splitting their time between demanding careers and an active social calendar that makes the most of the suburb's proximity to ferries, cafes and waterfront parks. Our regular domestic cleaning service is designed to keep your home consistently spotless without requiring any effort on your part. We offer weekly, fortnightly and monthly schedules, and our cleaners work through a thorough process covering all living areas, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens and floors. We bring all products and equipment with us, using eco-friendly cleaning agents that are effective on every surface without leaving harsh chemical residues behind.

End of Lease and Property Preparation

Neutral Bay's competitive rental market means that properties need to be presented in impeccable condition, whether you are a tenant vacating and wanting your full bond back or a landlord preparing a property for new occupants. Our end of lease cleaning service covers every detail that Neutral Bay property managers look for, from oven interiors and exhaust fans to tile grout, light fittings and balcony surfaces. We provide a bond-back guarantee with every end of lease clean, and we will return at no additional cost to address any agent concerns.
