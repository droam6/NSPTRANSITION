Cleaning in Pymble | North Shore Projects

Comprehensive Cleaning for Pymble's Large Estate Homes

Pymble is one of Sydney's most prestigious upper North Shore suburbs, characterised by expansive estate homes set on generous blocks, mature gardens and quiet, leafy streets that exude an atmosphere of understated elegance. Many Pymble properties feature five or more bedrooms, multiple living zones, formal dining rooms, home offices, media rooms and resort-style outdoor entertaining areas. Keeping a home of this scale consistently clean requires a dedicated, experienced cleaning team, and that is exactly what North Shore Projects provides. Our Pymble cleaning service is designed for larger homes that need more than a quick tidy, delivering a thorough, systematic clean that covers every room from top to bottom.

Our regular domestic cleaning packages are the most popular choice among Pymble families. We offer flexible scheduling on a weekly, fortnightly or monthly basis, and each visit follows a detailed room-by-room checklist customised to your home's layout. Our cleaners handle vacuuming and mopping all floors, sanitising bathrooms and ensuites, kitchen bench and appliance cleaning, dusting of all surfaces including shelving and skirting boards, and general tidying of bedrooms and living areas. For homes with multiple bathrooms, we ensure every shower, basin and toilet is left sparkling after each visit.

Entertaining Prep Cleaning

Pymble homeowners are known for their love of entertaining, and the suburb's generous properties are perfectly suited to hosting dinner parties, family gatherings and holiday celebrations. Our entertaining prep cleaning service ensures your home is in pristine condition before guests arrive. We focus on the areas that matter most when hosting: a spotless kitchen, gleaming bathrooms, polished floors, freshly cleaned cushions and throws, and a general atmosphere of effortless presentation. Many of our Pymble clients book this service ahead of Christmas, Easter and important family milestones.

Outdoor Area Cleaning

The generous outdoor spaces that define Pymble properties also require regular attention. Our outdoor cleaning service covers alfresco dining areas, patio furniture, pool surrounds, barbecue stations and outdoor kitchens. We remove cobwebs from pergolas and covered areas, sweep and mop outdoor tiles, wipe down furniture and cushions, and clean barbecue surfaces so your outdoor areas are always ready for use. Combined with regular indoor cleaning, this service ensures your entire Pymble property is maintained to a consistently high standard.
