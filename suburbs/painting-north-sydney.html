Painting in North Sydney | North Shore Projects

Premium Painting for North Sydney High-Rises & Offices

North Sydney is the commercial heart of the lower North Shore, home to towering office buildings, luxury high-rise apartments, and prestigious penthouse residences with sweeping views of Sydney Harbour and the CBD skyline. The painting demands in North Sydney are unique, requiring a team that is experienced with large-scale projects, high-end finishes, and the logistical challenges that come with working in multi-storey buildings. At North Shore Projects, we deliver painting services that match the calibre of North Sydney's premium properties, from sophisticated residential interiors to professional commercial office fitouts.

High-Rise Apartment & Penthouse Painting

North Sydney's residential towers offer some of the most spectacular living spaces on the North Shore, and the interiors need to match the views. Our painters specialise in high-rise apartment painting, understanding the specific requirements of working at height, including building access protocols, lift coordination, and the need for low-odour, fast-drying paints that minimise disruption in enclosed environments. For penthouse residences, we bring a luxury approach to every detail, from perfectly smooth wall finishes and crisp ceiling lines to specialty treatments on feature walls, built-in cabinetry, and architectural elements. We use premium products from Dulux, Taubmans, and specialty ranges to achieve the refined, flawless results that North Sydney penthouse owners expect.

Commercial Office Painting

A well-maintained office environment directly impacts employee productivity and the impression your business makes on clients. North Shore Projects provides professional commercial painting services for North Sydney offices of all sizes, from boutique suites to entire floors of commercial towers. We work outside business hours, on weekends, and during holiday periods to ensure zero disruption to your operations. Our commercial painting service covers walls, ceilings, trim, doors, columns, and reception areas, and we offer colour consultation to help you create a workspace that aligns with your brand identity and promotes a positive working environment.

Body Corporate & Strata Projects

North Sydney's numerous apartment buildings require regular maintenance painting to protect common areas and maintain property values. Our team works closely with strata managers and body corporate committees to plan and execute painting programs that cover lobbies, hallways, stairwells, car parks, and building exteriors. We provide comprehensive scoping, detailed quotations, and project timelines that give committees confidence in the process. Our painters are experienced with the scale and coordination required for body corporate work and always deliver on time and within the agreed budget.
